# WellQC+ Investor Pitch Script
### 10-Minute Seed Round Presentation — Word-for-Word Founder Script

---

## (0:00–1:00 — Opening: The Problem)

*What you're trying to achieve: Make every investor in the room feel the pain before you offer the cure. You want them nodding — either because they work in oil & gas and know this problem, or because they recognize the absurdity of it.*

---

[LOOK UP FROM NOTES] [SMILE]

"Let me start with a question.

Nigeria is Africa's largest oil producer. We are sitting on some of the most valuable subsurface data on the continent. And every single day, petroleum engineers across this country are making million-dollar decisions — drilling decisions, reservoir decisions, production decisions — based on data files.

Those files are called LAS files. They're the standard format for well log data. Every well drilled produces them. Every petrophysicist depends on them.

[PAUSE]

Here's the problem: those files are frequently broken.

Null values in the wrong place. Depth inversions. Missing curves. Corrupt headers. And the engineer doesn't always know — not until they're three hours deep into an analysis and something doesn't add up.

[PAUSE]

Right now, the standard solution is a manual review. A trained petrophysicist — someone earning serious money — sits down and eyeballs each file. They run scripts in Python. They check spreadsheets. They do this for every file, every well, every project.

The average petrophysicist QCs thirty LAS files a month. That's hours of high-skill time doing low-skill work.

That is the problem. And nobody has built a proper fix for it — not here in Nigeria, not for the African market."

## (1:00–2:00 — The Solution)

*What you're trying to achieve: Land the product concept cleanly. No jargon overload. Investors should be able to repeat what WellQC+ does in one sentence after this section.*

---

"So we built WellQC+.

WellQC+ is an AI-powered SaaS platform that automatically quality-checks LAS well log files — in seconds, not hours.

An engineer uploads their file. Our system runs over forty validation checks: depth integrity, curve completeness, null value distribution, header compliance, unit consistency. It flags every issue, scores the file, and gives a detailed report the engineer can act on immediately.

[PAUSE]

What used to take two to three hours of manual work — WellQC+ does in under sixty seconds.

[LOOK UP FROM NOTES]

And because we know that a lot of our users work on rig sites — with limited internet — we built WellQC+ as a Progressive Web App. It works offline. You can install it on your device and use it in the field, just like a native app.

It's live right now at wellqc.vercel.app. Not a demo. Not a waitlist. A working, production-ready product — with a hundred and thirty-one passing automated tests to prove it.

We are not coming to you with an idea. We are coming to you with a built, tested, working platform."

# Likely Investor Questions & Answers

*Prepare these cold. Know every answer without looking at notes.*

---

**Q1: What stops a big player like Schlumberger or Halliburton from building this and crushing you?**

"Great question. First — they haven't built it in fifty years of operating in this space, which tells you something. Second — even if they did, they would price it in dollars, deploy it for IOC enterprise budgets, and completely ignore the independent operators, small service companies, and mid-level engineers who make up our core market. We're building the affordable, accessible, African-first option. That's a positioning moat, not just a product moat. And by the time we're on their radar, we'll have the user base, the data, and the contracts that make us an acquisition target — not a casualty."

---

**Q2: Why would someone pay ₦75,000 a month for this when they could write a Python script themselves?**

"Because writing a Python script takes time they don't have, maintaining it takes skill they may not have, and it still doesn't give them a shareable report, an audit trail, or a quality score their manager can review. WellQC+ isn't competing with Python — it's competing with the three hours of senior engineer time that a manual QC process consumes. At ₦75,000 per month, if WellQC+ saves one engineer two hours per week, it has already paid for itself in salary cost alone. The ROI conversation is easy."

---

**Q3: You have 131 tests and a live product — but do you have paying customers yet?**

"We are in active onboarding conversations with early adopters from our beta program. The product launched recently and we are in our early commercial stage — which is exactly the right time to bring in seed funding to accelerate growth. What I can tell you is that the product is production-ready, the payment infrastructure is live, and we have not had to tell a single early user 'sorry, come back later.' We are ready to convert."

---

**Q4: What's your customer acquisition strategy — how do you actually get engineers to find you?**

"Three channels. First — community: we're active in Nigerian petrophysics and geoscience communities, LinkedIn groups, NAPE events, SPE Nigeria. These are tight-knit professionals who share tools they trust. Second — content: SEO-driven technical content targeting 'LAS file QC', 'well log quality check' — engineers search for solutions to this problem, and we want to be the first result they find. Third — direct outreach: we are mapping the service companies and independent operators in Nigeria and doing direct business development. The ₦5 million marketing allocation in this round is specifically to execute that playbook."

---

**Q5: What happens if the oil price crashes and companies cut software budgets?**

"₦75,000 a month is the price of one dinner meeting for a petroleum engineer. Even in a downturn, companies don't cut tools that prevent expensive mistakes — they cut nice-to-haves. WellQC+ is not a nice-to-have. Bad well log data causes bad reservoir models, which causes bad drilling decisions. The cost of one bad well decision dwarfs our annual subscription by several orders of magnitude. If anything, downturns make companies more rigorous about data quality, not less."

---

**Q6: How defensible is this — what's your moat?**

"Three things build our moat over time. First — data: every file we process teaches our AI models to flag issues more accurately. That's a compound advantage. Second — network effects: when teams at a company adopt WellQC+, they share reports internally. Usage spreads naturally within organisations. Third — switching costs: once a company's quality control workflow is built around WellQC+ — with their historical reports, their audit trails, their standard templates — switching has real friction. We're not building a commodity tool. We're becoming infrastructure."

---

**Q7: Why raise ₦20 million specifically — why not more, or less?**

"Less wouldn't get us through the engineering milestones we need — multi-region infrastructure and Kubernetes deployment are prerequisites for enterprise contracts. More would dilute at a stage where we can achieve profitability and demonstrate strong metrics before a Series A at a much better valuation. ₦20 million is the precise amount that lets us hit 10,000 MAU, achieve profitability, close our first enterprise pilot, and raise a Series A from a position of strength — not desperation."

---

**Q8: What does success look like in 18 months — what are your key milestones?**

"Eighteen months from now: 10,000 monthly active users, 500 paying Pro customers, ₦23 million or more in monthly recurring profit, at least one signed enterprise pilot contract with an IOC or major service company, SOC 2 certified, and infrastructure deployed across at least three African regions. That's the milestone set that validates both our product-market fit and our enterprise readiness — and positions us perfectly for a Series A conversation."
